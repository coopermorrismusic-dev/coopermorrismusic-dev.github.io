About Cooper

Music with heart—and a little room to dance.

Cooper Morris is a singer-songwriter and live musician based in Eugene, Oregon. He has performed at wineries, breweries, private events, and venues around the area since 2017.

His sets move through folk, blues, jazz, and pop, mixing familiar songs with original music. Cooper performs solo, as a duo with his sister Jen Sennett, and with local bands for events that call for a bigger sound.
